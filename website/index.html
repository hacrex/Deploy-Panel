01

Outbound-only agents

Nodes dial the control plane over mTLS. There is no inbound panel port to expose and no long-lived SSH key to lose.

02

Secrets stay on the host

Site secrets are encrypted with a master key the panel never leaves home without. Back it up separately from the database.

03

Every action is audited

Who deployed, who restored, who changed a limit. The audit log is part of the core, not a paid module.

04

Backups that restore

A backup is only a backup once it has been restored. DeployPanel can restore each one into a scratch Site and prove it.

Is DeployPanel free?

Yes. DeployPanel is open source under Apache-2.0. Self-host it on your own servers. Optional modules and managed services may be offered later, but the core panel stays yours.

What can I deploy?

At v0.1: Dockerfile, Compose and image Sites. PHP, WordPress, Node, Python, static Sites, Git webhooks and git push follow through v0.2. Every one of them is managed as a Site.

Do I need my own server?

Yes, a Linux host with systemd. The panel listens on loopback by default and expects to sit behind your reverse proxy with TLS. Nodes run the outbound-only agent.

How do agents connect?

Agents dial the control plane outbound over mTLS. You do not open an inbound panel port on each node and the panel does not hold SSH keys to your servers.

Can I import from CapRover, Coolify or CyberPanel?

Importers are on the v1.0 roadmap, not in the current scaffold. The plan is to import against real exports collected during v0.1 to v0.3, so moving in is not a rewrite.

Is it production ready?

No. The repository is at week 1 of v0.1: config, SQLite migrations, health and auth endpoints. The v0.1 exit criteria are a fresh VPS to a deployed app in under 10 minutes, under 150 MB idle RAM and no public admin port.
